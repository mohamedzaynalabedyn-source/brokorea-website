How it works

Trading from Korea, step by step. You tell us what you need, and we handle sourcing, supplier communication, preparation and shipping coordination.

  1. 1

    Customer inquiry

    Send vehicle details, a parts list or your requirements.

  2. 2

    Sourcing in Korea

    We research available suppliers and options.

  3. 3

    Quotation & confirmation

    We send pricing, availability, condition and other relevant details.

  4. 4

    Order approval

    You confirm the order and the agreed terms.

  5. 5

    Preparation

    We coordinate purchasing, packaging and the necessary documents.

  6. 6

    Shipping coordination

    We arrange or support shipping according to your destination and the agreed service.

What to send us

  • For vehicles: make, model, year range, condition and any required specifications
  • For parts: part numbers and quantities, one per line, or an Excel/PDF file
  • No part number? The VIN (chassis number), make, model and year, plus photos of the part
  • Destination country and city or port

Frequently asked questions

Why are there no prices on the website?

Prices and availability in Korea change often, so we quote each request individually.

Do you trade vehicles as well as parts?

Yes. We help customers buy new and used vehicles from Korea, subject to availability and agreed specifications. Tell us the make, model, year range and your requirements.

I don’t have the part numbers. Can you still help?

Yes. Send the VIN (chassis number), the car make, model and year, and a photo of the part or its label, and we will work to identify the correct part.

Do you supply genuine or aftermarket parts?

Both, depending on what is available for your request. Tell us your preference and we will confirm the options in our quotation.

Which countries do you work with?

We serve customers worldwide from our base in Korea. Shipping options depend on the destination’s import rules and the agreed service, so tell us your country and city or port.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are agreed for each order and confirmed before we start purchasing.

How do I send a long list?

Paste it into the quote form (one part per line), or email your Excel or PDF file to info@brokorea.com.

Tell us what you need from Korea.

Vehicles, automotive parts or trading support: use the form, email us, or message us on WhatsApp.
