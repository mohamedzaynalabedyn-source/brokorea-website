How it works

Ordering from Korea is simple. You tell us what you need — we do the sourcing, checking, packing and shipping.

  1. 1

    Send part numbers or VIN

    Send your list of part numbers and quantities. No part numbers? Send the VIN (chassis number) and a photo of the part.

  2. 2

    We source in Korea

    We check genuine and OEM availability with our suppliers across Korea.

  3. 3

    We send your quote

    You receive a clear quote with prices, availability and lead time.

  4. 4

    We ship to your country

    After confirmation and payment, we pack your order for export and ship it to Iraq, the UAE or your country.

What to send us

  • Part numbers and quantities — one per line, or an Excel/PDF file
  • No part number? The VIN (chassis number), make, model and year
  • Photos of the old part or its label help us match it exactly
  • Destination country and city or port

Frequently asked questions

Why are there no prices on the website?

Prices and availability of genuine parts change often, so we quote each request individually to give you accurate, current prices.

I don't have the part numbers. Can you still help?

Yes. Send the VIN (chassis number), the car make, model and year, and a photo of the part or its label. We'll identify the correct part number.

Do you supply genuine or aftermarket parts?

Both. For Hyundai, Kia and Genesis we supply genuine Hyundai Mobis parts, and we can also offer OEM and quality aftermarket options. Tell us your preference in the notes.

Which countries do you ship to?

Mainly Iraq and the United Arab Emirates. We can also ship to other countries — just ask.

How do I send a long list?

Paste it into the quote form (one part per line), or email your Excel or PDF file to info@brokorea.com.

Have a parts list? Send it now.

Paste your part numbers into the form, email your Excel file, or send it on WhatsApp.
