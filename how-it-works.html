How it works

Tell us what you need. We research suppliers and options in Korea, confirm the details with you, then prepare your order and coordinate shipping.

  1. 1

    Customer inquiry

    Vehicle details, a parts list or your requirements.

  2. 2

    Sourcing in Korea

    We research available suppliers and options.

  3. 3

    Quotation & confirmation

    Pricing, availability, condition and key details.

  4. 4

    Order approval

    You confirm the order and the agreed terms.

  5. 5

    Preparation

    Purchasing, packaging and the necessary documents.

  6. 6

    Shipping coordination

    Arranged or supported for your destination.

What to send us

  • For parts: part numbers and quantities, one per line, or an Excel/PDF file
  • No part number? The VIN (chassis number), make, model and year
  • For vehicles: the make, model, year and specifications you are looking for
  • Your destination country and city or port

Frequently asked questions

Why are there no prices on the website?

Prices and availability change often, so we quote each request individually.

Do you also supply vehicles?

Yes. We handle requests for new and used vehicles from Korea, subject to availability and agreed specifications.

I don't have the part numbers. Can you still help?

Yes. Send the VIN (chassis number), the car make, model and year, and a photo of the part or its label, and we'll identify the part you need.

Do you supply genuine or aftermarket parts?

Both. For Hyundai, Kia and Genesis we can source genuine Hyundai Mobis parts, and we can also offer OEM and aftermarket options. Tell us your preference in the notes.

Which countries do you serve?

We serve customers worldwide from South Korea. Tell us your destination and we'll confirm the shipping options for your order.

How are payment terms handled?

Payment terms are agreed for each order.

How do I send a long list?

Paste it into the quote form (one part per line), or email your Excel or PDF file to info@brokorea.com.
