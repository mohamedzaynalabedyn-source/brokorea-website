Engine parts

Internal and external engine components for petrol and diesel engines — from gaskets and seals to timing kits, and complete engine assemblies on request.

Commonly ordered

  • Pistons, piston rings and liners
  • Cylinder head gaskets and full gasket sets
  • Timing chains, belts, tensioners and guides
  • Oil pumps and oil seals
  • Valves, lifters and camshafts
  • Engine mounts
  • Turbochargers
  • Long blocks and engine assemblies (on request)

These are examples only. We can look for any part in this category in Korea, subject to availability, including parts for Mercedes-Benz and BMW on request.

To get a fast quote, send us

  • Part numbers and quantities (one per line)
  • Or the VIN / chassis number with car make, model and year
  • A photo of the old part or its label, if you have it
  • Your preference: genuine or aftermarket
