Body parts

Exterior and interior body parts. Large panels are quoted with export packing and shipping in mind.

Commonly ordered

  • Front and rear bumpers and reinforcements
  • Grilles, fenders and hoods
  • Doors, tailgates and trunk lids
  • Side mirrors and mirror glass
  • Mouldings, clips and trim
  • Radiator supports and front panels

These are examples only — we can source any part in this category that is available in Korea.

To get a fast quote, send us

  • Part numbers and quantities (one per line)
  • Or the VIN / chassis number with car make, model and year
  • A photo of the old part or its label, if you have it
  • Your preference: genuine or aftermarket
